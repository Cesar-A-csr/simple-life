## What is the game about?

It's a daily life simulation game for consoles. Your goal is to manage your money and food on a daily basis to avoid going bankrupt or starving.

## Starting the Game.

When you start the application, you will be asked for your character's name. The game will not progress if you leave this field blank.
At the start, the game will randomly generate:

- Your monthly rent (between $100 and $300).
- Your bi-weekly salary (between $275 and $365).
- Your starting money: $365.

## Daily Dynamics.

Each day of the game follows a fixed sequence. To advance between screens, you must press the Enter key.

[Start of Day] ➔ [Time Information] ➔ [Player Status] ➔ [Market] ➔ [Purchase Phase] ➔ [End of Day]

A. Data
- Time: Displays the current day of the month, as well as the total number of days, weeks, and months played.
- Player: Displays your current balance, hunger level, and allocated expenses/income.
- Market: Lists the fruits and vegetables available today, with their prices, quantities, and the satiety level they provide.

B. Purchase Phase
- Choose the number of the product you wish to purchase (for example: 1 for apple).
- Enter the quantity you wish to purchase. You cannot enter negative numbers, zero, or a quantity greater than what is available in the store.

## Economic and Health Rules

- Rent Collection: On the 1st of each month, your set rent will be automatically deducted.
- Salary Payment: You will receive your salary payment on the 15th and 30th of each month.
- Energy and Hunger Consumption: You spend between 25 and 50 energy points every day.
- Eating restores energy based on the food chosen (Satiety x Quantity).
- If you don't eat enough, your hunger level will increase.

## How to Win or Lose?

- Loss by Bankruptcy: If your money reaches $0 or a negative value at the end of the day.
- Loss by Hunger: If your hunger level reaches or exceeds 100 points.
- Advancing to the Next Month: On day 30, the game will ask if you wish to continue playing or quit. If you choose to quit, the game will end safely.

## General System Architecture

The code implements a continuous loop simulation game (Game Loop) using the Python console. It manages state using simple global variables (dictionaries for mutable attributes) and interactions via standard input/output (input() / print()).

The general flow is summarized as follows:

- Initialization: Reading and validating the character's name; initializing player state and time.
- Main Loop (while True):
- Generating market inventory (dynamic values ​​using random).
- Mapping and displaying data.
- Capturing purchase decisions with strict type and range validation.
- Recalculating attributes (money, hunger, time).
- Checking for game-ending or optional stop conditions.

## Key Function and Algorithm Logic

Input Validation (Player Name)

Uses .strip() to discard whitespace and the not operator to force a valid name before proceeding.

```python
nombre_jugador = input("Ingresa el nombre de tu personaje: ").strip()
while not nombre_jugador:
    nombre_jugador = input("Opción invalida, no puede estar vacío el nombre. Ingresa el nombre de tu personaje: ").strip()
```

Hunger Parameter Modification

Hunger is calculated by subtracting energy recovered from energy spent randomly during the day, preventing negative values ​​using max():

```python
dict_jugador["hambre"] = max(0, dict_jugador["hambre"] + energia_gastada - energia_recuperada)
```

## Game Over Conditions
The main while loop terminates execution under three circumstances:

- Voluntary decision: continue != 's' upon completion of day 30 (one month).
- Bankruptcy: dict_jugador["dinero"] <= 0.
- Physical collapse: dict_jugador["hambre"] >= 100.
